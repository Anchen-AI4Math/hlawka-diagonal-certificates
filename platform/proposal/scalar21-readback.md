For every real number $p$ with $21\le p\le26$ (including both endpoints), let $C(p)$ be the real supremum
$$C(p)=\sup\left\{\frac{3(t^p+2)^{1/p}-3^{1/p}|2-t|}{6(t^p+2)^{1/p}-3\bigl(2|1-t|^p+2^p\bigr)^{1/p}}:\ t\in\mathbb R,\ \frac12\le t\le2\right\}.$$
Then all three inequalities hold simultaneously:
$$\frac{273}{625}p<C(p),\qquad C(p)\le\frac p2,\qquad \frac{1-\frac{1011}{2500}}{2\left(1-\left(\frac{1+(\frac{1011}{2500})^p}{2}\right)^{1/p}\right)}<C(p).$$
Here every power is a real power, $|\cdot|$ is the absolute value on $\mathbb R$, and division has the total real-number convention $a/0=0$.
