For every real number $p\ge 21$, let $N_{p,n}(x)=\bigl(\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}|x_i|^p\bigr)^{1/p}$ for each natural number $n$ and each vector $x\in\mathbb C^n$, where $|x_i|$ is the complex modulus, and define $A_p(t)=(t^p+2)^{1/p}$, $B_p(t)=(2|1-t|^p+2^p)^{1/p}$, and $K_p=\sup\bigl\{\frac{3A_p(t)-3^{1/p}|2-t|}{6A_p(t)-3B_p(t)}:t\in[\tfrac12,2]\bigr\}$. Then $K_p$ is the least real number $C$ such that, for every natural number $n$ and all $x,y,z\in\mathbb C^n$, $N_{p,n}(x)+N_{p,n}(y)+N_{p,n}(z)-N_{p,n}(x+y+z)\le C\bigl[(N_{p,n}(x)+N_{p,n}(y)-N_{p,n}(x+y))+(N_{p,n}(x)+N_{p,n}(z)-N_{p,n}(x+z))+(N_{p,n}(y)+N_{p,n}(z)-N_{p,n}(y+z))\bigr]$. Explicitly, this inequality holds with $C=K_p$ simultaneously for every such dimension and triple, and $K_p\le C$ for every real $C$ for which it holds simultaneously for every dimension and triple; the competing constants $C$ have no separately imposed sign restriction. The quantifiers include zero vectors and $n=0$: in dimension zero the coordinate sum is empty, $N_{p,0}=0$, and the required inequality is $0\le 0$ for every real $C$.
