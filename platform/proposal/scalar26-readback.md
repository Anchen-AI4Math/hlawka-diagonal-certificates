For every real number $p$ satisfying $26\le p\le32$, define $C_p=\sup\left\{\dfrac{3(t^p+2)^{1/p}-3^{1/p}|2-t|}{6(t^p+2)^{1/p}-3\bigl(2|1-t|^p+2^p\bigr)^{1/p}}\;\middle|\;t\in\mathbb R,\ \dfrac12\le t\le2\right\}$. Then all three inequalities $\dfrac{443}{1000}p<C_p$, $C_p\le\dfrac p2$, and $\dfrac{1-\frac{197}{500}}{2\left(1-\left(\frac{1+(\frac{197}{500})^p}{2}\right)^{1/p}\right)}<C_p$ hold simultaneously; all powers and divisions are taken in the real numbers, with division by zero interpreted as zero.
